### **2. Situational Irony**

- **Point 1: Global vs. Local Empathy Paradox**
    - `"You need some perspective Nabs. You keep forgetting about the Syrian refugees, how little they have, and how lucky you are."`
    - `"We stayed at the Four Seasons in Damascus... I guess your cousin wasn't staying there too?"`

    - `"I thought you'd be able to step outside yourself and empathise with others—"`
    - `"To think we spent so much time working on our project... We didn't hear from her again."`

    - `"As Nabilah had said before, Margarine belonged to a different world. Regardless of race, language or religion. But what about money?"`
    - `"I guess that's where people like you go to school," Nabilah said.`
