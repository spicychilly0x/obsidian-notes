
**1. Respiration vs. Breathing**

- **Respiration**: The **oxidation of food** (primarily glucose) to release energy in living cells.
- **Breathing**: A **mechanical ventilation** process that supplies oxygen needed for respiration and removes waste gases.

**2. Cellular Respiration: Aerobic vs. Anaerobic**

- **Aerobic Respiration**:
    - **Condition**: Occurs in the presence of oxygen (O2​).
    - **Location**: Cytoplasm and mitochondria.
    - **Equation**: C6​H12​O6​+6O2​→6CO2​+6H2​O + **Large amount of ATP**.
- **Anaerobic Respiration**:
    - **Condition**: Occurs in the absence of oxygen.
    - **Outcome**: Incomplete breakdown of glucose; releases **small amounts of ATP**.
    - **Two Types**:
        1. **Alcoholic Fermentation (Yeast)**: Glucose→Ethanol+CO2​ + Small ATP.
        2. **Lactic Acid Fermentation (Human Muscle)**: Glucose→Lactic Acid + **2 ATP**.

**3. Anaerobic Respiration in Muscles (Oxygen Debt)**

- **Mechanism**: During vigorous exercise, the O2​ demand exceeds the supply despite increased breathing/heart rates. Muscles respire anaerobically to provide extra energy.
- **Consequences**: Lactic acid builds up; high concentrations are **toxic**, causing **muscular fatigue, pain, and stiffness**.
- **Recovery**:
    1. Increased/deep breathing continues after exercise to **"repay" the oxygen debt**.
    2. Lactic acid is transported to the **liver**.
    3. In the liver: Some lactic acid is oxidized for energy; that energy converts the rest back into **glucose** (later stored as **glycogen** in muscles).

**4. Anatomy of the Respiratory System**

- **Air Path (Memorize Order)**: External nostril → Nasal passages → Pharynx → Larynx → Trachea → Bronchi → Bronchioles → Alveoli (air sacs).
- **Conditioning of Air**: Before reaching lungs, air is:
    - **Warmed** to body temperature.
    - **Moistened** by mucus.
    - **Filtered**: **Goblet cells** secrete mucus to trap dust/bacteria; **Cilia** (hair-like structures) sweep mucus toward the pharynx to be swallowed or coughed out.

**5. Mechanism of Ventilation (Breathing)**

|Feature|**Inhalation (Inspiration)**|**Exhalation (Expiration)**|
|---|---|---|
|**Diaphragm**|**Contracts** and flattens.|**Relaxes** and arches upward.|
|**External Intercostals**|**Contract**.|Relax.|
|**Internal Intercostals**|Relax.|**Contract**.|
|**Rib Cage**|Moves **up and out**.|Moves **down and in**.|
|**Thoracic Volume**|**Increases**.|**Decreases**.|
|**Air Pressure**|Inside lungs **decreases** (below atmospheric).|Inside lungs **increases** (above atmospheric).|
|**Air Flow**|Rushes **into** the lungs.|Forced **out** of the lungs.|

**6. Gaseous Exchange in Alveoli**

- **Adaptations for Efficiency**:
    1. **Large Surface Area**: Millions of alveoli.
    2. **Thin Walls**: Only **one cell thick** for a short diffusion pathway.
    3. **Moist Surface**: Layer of water allows gases to dissolve and diffuse in solution.
    4. **Rich Capillary Supply**: Maintains a **steep concentration gradient** for rapid diffusion.
- **Gas Transport**:
    - **Oxygen**: 98% bound to **haemoglobin** (HbO2​); 2% dissolved in plasma.
    - **Carbon Dioxide**: 70% as **hydrogen carbonate ions (**HCO3−​**)** in plasma (facilitated by **carbonic anhydrase** in RBCs); 23% as carbamino-haemoglobin; 7% dissolved in plasma.

**7. Lung Volumes & Composition**

- **Inspired vs. Expired Air**: O2​ (21% → 16%); CO2​ (0.03% → 4%); N2​ (78% remains 78%); Temperature (variable → 37°C).
- **Key Volumes**:
    - **Tidal Volume (TV)**: Air moved in/out during normal breath.
    - **Vital Capacity**: Max air forcibly expired after deep inspiration (TV+IRV+ERV).
    - **Residual Volume (RV)**: Air remaining in lungs after strenuous expiration (prevents lung collapse).

**8. Effects of Tobacco Smoke**

- **Nicotine**: Addictive; releases **adrenaline** (increases heart rate/BP); makes blood clot easily (stroke/heart attack risk).
- **Carbon Monoxide**: Competes with O2​ for haemoglobin (**carboxyhaemoglobin**); promotes **atherosclerosis** (narrowing of arteries).
- **Tar**: Carcinogenic (cancer-causing); **paralyses cilia**, leading to blocked airways and higher infection risk.
- **Chronic Bronchitis**: Inflammation of epithelium; **excessive mucus** secretion; persistent cough.
- **Emphysema**: Violent coughing **destroys alveoli walls**, reducing surface area for gas exchange; lungs lose elasticity; causes breathlessness.
- **COPD**: The combination of chronic bronchitis and emphysema.
