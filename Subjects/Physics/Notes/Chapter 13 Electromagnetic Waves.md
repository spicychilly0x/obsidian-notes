**1. Fundamental Properties of EM Waves**

- **Wave Type:** All electromagnetic (EM) waves are **transverse waves**.
- **Vacuum Propagation:** They do **not require a medium** and can travel through a vacuum.
- **Electric Charge/Deflection:** They do not carry an electric charge and are **not deflected by magnets**.
- **Speed:** Every member of the EM spectrum travels at the **exact same speed in a vacuum or air**.
    - **Value to Memorize:** $3.0 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$ (or $300,000,000\text{ m/s}$).
- **Energy Equation:** Governed by $E = hf$ (where $E$ is energy in Joules, $h$ is Planck's constant $6.626 \times 10^{-34}\text{ J Hz}^{-1}$ , and $f$ is frequency in Hz).
    - **Relationship:** Energy is directly proportional to frequency.

**2. The Electromagnetic Spectrum (Order & Trends)**

- **The Continuous Spectrum (Longest to Shortest Wavelength):** (raging martians invade venus using xray guns) $$\text{Radio Waves} \rightarrow \text{Microwaves} \rightarrow \text{Infrared (IR)} \rightarrow \text{Visible Light} \rightarrow \text{Ultraviolet (UV)} \rightarrow \text{X-rays} \rightarrow \text{Gamma Rays}$$
- **Key Trends to Memorize:**
    - **Moving from Radio to Gamma:** Frequency and energy **increase**, while wavelength **decreases**.
    - **Radio Waves:** Have the **longest wavelength**, lowest frequency, and lowest energy.
    - **Gamma Rays:** Have the **shortest wavelength**, highest frequency, and highest energy.
- **The Visible Spectrum:** The only part of the EM spectrum visible to the human eye without special equipment.
    - **Color Order (from longest to shortest wavelength):** Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Indigo, Violet.
    - **Red:** Longest wavelength, lowest frequency, lowest energy.
    - **Violet:** Shortest wavelength, highest frequency, highest energy.

**3. Radiation Detectors**

- **Radio & Microwaves:** Aerial, Radio Telescope.
- **Infrared:** Thermometer, Skin.
- **Visible Light:** Eyes, Solar Cell (converts visible light energy into electrical energy).
- **Ultraviolet & X-Rays:** Photographic Film.
- **Gamma Rays:** Geiger-Müller (GM) Tube and counter (detects ionising radiation).

**4. Practical Applications (High-Yield Exam Matches)**

- **Radio Waves:** Wireless radio transmission, terrestrial TV signals, and RFID tags. _(Note: Radio waves are EM waves, NOT sound waves!)_
- **Microwaves:** Microwave ovens (cooking food), speaking on mobile phones, satellite television, Wi-Fi, and GPS. _(Note: Satellite TV communication uses microwaves, not radio waves)._
- **Infrared (IR):** Television remote controllers, intruder alarms, electric heaters, toasters, thermal imaging cameras (for police/fire services), and physiotherapy (treating muscle injuries).
- **Visible Light:** Sight, optical fibres for telecommunications and medical uses.
- **Ultraviolet (UV):** Sunbeds, sterilization, anti-forgery (security markings on property), treating skin conditions, and producing Vitamin D3.
- **X-Rays:** Medical imaging/radiology (checking for broken bones) and scanning airport luggage.
- **Gamma Rays:** Radiotherapy (cancer treatment), radioactive tracers in medicine, leak detection in underground pipes, and sterilizing hospital equipment.

**5. Hazards and Safety Precautions**

- **Ionising Effects (UV, X-rays, Gamma):**  (high-frequency, high-energy electromagnetic waves interact with the atoms or molecules inside living cells to **produce ions**)
    - **The Mechanism:** High-frequency, high-energy waves interact with atoms/molecules in cells to produce ions. These ions disrupt normal cell behavior, which can cause **cell death, mutations, diseases (like cancer), or organ failure**.
    - **X-Ray Safety:** Radiographers must stand behind **lead-glass screens** to reduce exposure to radiation.
    - **Gamma Safety:** Never handle radioactive sources with bare hands; use **tongs or forceps**. Never point sources at the body, especially the eyes.
    - **UV Safety:** Over-exposure over time causes skin cancer and premature aging. Protect yourself by using **sunblock** and other UV protection.
- **Heating Effects (Microwaves, IR, Visible):**
    - **Microwaves:** Have a lower frequency than visible light but **can** cause heating of internal body tissue.
    - **Infrared:** Too much exposure to heat radiation can **burn bare skin**.
    - **Visible Light:** Looking directly into bright sunlight can damage your sight; use protective gear like **sunglasses**.
