
**1. Energy Fundamentals**

- **Definition:** The capacity of a body to do work.
- **SI Unit:** Joule (**J**).
- **Energy Stores (Must Memorize):**
    - **Kinetic:** Energy of moving objects.
    - **Gravitational Potential (**Ep​**):** Energy due to height above ground (technically an object-Earth system store).
    - **Chemical Potential:** Found in food, batteries, and fossil fuels.
    - **Elastic Potential:** Found in stretched/compressed springs or rubber.
    - **Internal (Thermal):** All objects have this; hot objects have more.
    - **Nuclear:** Energy within the nuclei of atoms.

**2. <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Key Formulas</mark>**
[[Physics formulas]]
- **Kinetic Energy (Ek​**): 
$$
 Ek​= \frac{1}{2}​mv^2.
$$
    - m in kg; v in m/s.
- **Gravitational Potential Energy (**Ep​**):** 
$$
Ep​=mgh.
$$
    - g≈10 N/kg on Earth, h = height
- **Work Done (**W**):** 
$$
W=F×d
$$
    - d is the distance moved **in the direction of the force**.
    - If no distance is moved (e.g., holding a box at a constant height), work done is zero.
- **Power (**P**):** <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">rate at which energy is transferred</mark>
- P=tE​ or P=tW​.
    - Alternative Formula: P=F×v (Force × velocity).
    - SI Unit: Watt (**W**); 1 W=1 J/s.
- **Efficiency:** (Total energy inputUseful energy output​)×100%.

**3. Conservation and Transfer**

- **Principle of Conservation of Energy:** Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transferred from one store to another. Total energy in an isolated system remains constant.
- **Transfer Pathways:**
    - **Mechanically** (by a force).
    - **Heating** (due to temperature difference).
    - **Propagation of waves** (EM or sound).
    - **Electrically** (by electric current).

**4. Exam-Specific Logic & Problem Solving**

- **Work Against Friction:** Calculated as f×s (frictional force × distance along the surface).
- **Conservation of Mechanical Energy:** In a smooth (frictionless) system, Ep1​+Ek1​=Ep2​+Ek2​.
- **Systems with Friction:**
    - **Gain in** Ek​ = Ep​ lost−Work done against friction.
    - **Work done against friction** = Ek​ lost when an object slides to a stop on rough ground.
- **Steady Speed on a Slope:** If an object moves at a constant speed up a slope, the work input equals the **increase in** Ep​ **+ work done against friction** (no work is used to increase Ek​).
- **Common Assumptions:** For ideal calculations, assume no energy is lost to air resistance or the internal stores of the surroundings.
- **Power & Velocity:** Maximum power occurs when an object is at its highest velocity if the applied force is constant.
