
**1. Centre of Gravity (CG)**

- **Definition:** The single point where the entire **weight** of a body appears to act.
- **Regular Shapes:** For uniform, regular objects (e.g., circles, squares), the CG lies at the geometric **center**.
- **Key Property:** The CG does not need to be within the solid part of the body (e.g., the center of a **ring** or **horseshoe**).
- **Experimental Finding:** If a body is hanging freely at rest, its CG is always located **vertically below the pivot**.

**2. Stability and Equilibrium**

- **Stability:** The ability of an object to return to its original position after being tilted slightly.
- **Types of Equilibrium:**
    - **Stable:** Tiling causes the CG to **rise**; the weight creates a moment that restores the object to its original position.
    - **Unstable:** Tilting causes the CG to **fall**; the weight creates a moment that causes the object to **topple**.
    - **Neutral:** Tilting leaves the CG at the **same level**; the object remains in its new position.
- **Toppling Rule:** An object topples when the **line of action of its weight** falls outside its base area.
- **Increasing Stability:**
    1. Lower the CG (e.g., add weight to the base).
    2. Widen the base area.

**3. Moment of a Force**

- **Definition:** The **turning effect** of a force. It is the product of the force and the **perpendicular distance** from the line of action of the force to the pivot.
- **Formula:** <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Moment=F×d</mark>.  [[Physics formulas]]
    - F**:** Force in Newtons (N).
    - d**:** Perpendicular distance in meters (m) or centimeters (cm).
- **SI Unit:** Newton-meter (Nm).
- **Direction:** Must be specified as **clockwise** or **anti-clockwise** about the pivot.
- **Zero Moment:** A force has no turning effect if its line of action passes directly **through the pivot** (distance = 0).

**4. Principle of Moments**

- **The Principle:** For a body in equilibrium, the **sum of clockwise moments** about a pivot is equal to the **sum of anti-clockwise moments** about the same pivot.
- **Conditions for Equilibrium:**
    1. **Resultant Force = 0:** Sum of upward forces = Sum of downward forces.
    2. **Resultant Moment = 0:** Sum of CW moments = Sum of ACW moments.

**5. Exam Problem-Solving Tips**

- **Uniform Objects:** The weight of a uniform rod or beam always acts at its **geometric midpoint**.
- **Non-Uniform Objects:** The weight acts at the **CG**, which is often shifted toward the "heavier" side.
- **Two-Pivot Problems:**
    1. Set up a force balance equation (RA​+RB​=Total Weight).
    2. Take moments about **one** of the pivots to eliminate one unknown reaction force (since its distance to that pivot is zero).
- **Biomechanical Problems (e.g., the Arm):** Treat the **elbow joint** as the pivot. Increasing the distance between the joint and muscle attachment provides a mechanical advantage by increasing the moment of the muscle force.
